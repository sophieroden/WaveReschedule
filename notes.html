Project Notes & Behind the Build

Module: Technology for Business (H7BTEC) | Assessment: CA2[cite: 1, 2]

Assigned Case: Case F: Atlantic Edge Surf School, Lahinch, Co. Clare[cite: 4]

Task 1: Think It Through Before You Build

Hunch (Before AI):

Owners of schools for surfing and outdoor activities in Ireland would pay for this system because it eliminates hours of rebooking by phone and helps prevent a loss of revenue from unbooked slots.

Choosing a Specific Niche

  • Niche: Independent surf schools and coastal adventure providers along the Wild Atlantic Way in Ireland
  • Who pays (The Buyer): The owner-operator or business manager
  • Who uses it every day (The User): Head instructors, front-desk staff, or owners managing bookings on-site
  • Pain (Invented): 'When a gale hits, I spend three solid hours on the phone and WhatsApp trying to reschedule 60 angry customers, and by the end of it, five of them just give up and demand refunds.'

Technology Type

  • Type of technology: Booking management and automated rebooking queue system
  • Real products/services:
    • Viking Bookings (Surf school and water sports management software built specifically around lessons, instructors and changing weather conditions) — Checked on 19th October 2026 at vikingbookings.com
    • Supersaas (Online appointment scheduling system) — Checked on 19th October 2026 at supersaas.com

Business Model & PRD

  • Business Model (Assumption): €150 set up fee and €49 monthly subscription fee. Reasoning: Small seasonal adventure operators have limited cash flow in Winter, so a low monthly fee paired with a modest onboarding fee aligns software costs directly with their active operating season.
  • PRD:
    • Hypothesis: Automated weather rebooking will reduce administrative cancellation handling time from 3 hours to under 10 minutes per weather event.
    • Audience: Independent outdoor adventure and surf school operators in Ireland
    • One call to action: Join the early-access waiting list
    • Measurement of Success: Conversion rate of visitors clicking the 'Try the demo' and submitting the waiting list form
    • Out of scope: Direct payment processing/refund gateways and automated SMS/WhatsApp API message dispatching

Task 2: Alpha Testing Log

  • What was tried: Tested the demo application on a mobile smartphone with simulated wet hands, cancelling an active surf session and checking if bookings moved into the rebook queue.
  • What broke/confused: Initially, when a session was cancelled, the live euro value counter did not update dynamically until the page was manually refreshed. Furthermore, touch targets felt slightly too close together on smaller iPhone screens.
  • What was changed: Updated the JavaScript DOM rendering function to trigger live recalculations instantly upon state change, and increased button minimum height to 55px with increased padding for easier wet-hand navigation.
